Introduction

NoteXP ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application NoteXP (the "App").

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address when you create an account
  • Content: Notices, announcements, and other content you create or share
  • Profile Information: Any profile details you choose to provide

Information We Collect Automatically

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, app version
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app, features used, time spent
  • Camera Access: Used only for QR code scanning to subscribe to notice boards
  • Storage Access: Used to cache notices for offline viewing

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your location data
  • We do not access your contacts or personal files
  • We do not track your browsing history outside the app
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • Enable QR code scanning for board subscriptions
  • Send you notifications about notices and updates
  • Improve the App's performance and user experience
  • Provide customer support
  • Ensure the security and integrity of our services

Camera Permission

The App requests camera permission solely for:

  • QR Code Scanning: To quickly subscribe to notice boards by scanning QR codes
  • No Photo/Video Storage: We do not store, save, or transmit any photos or videos
  • No Face Recognition: We do not use facial recognition or biometric data

Data Storage and Security

  • Secure Storage: Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption
  • Firebase: We use Google Firebase for secure data storage and authentication
  • Local Caching: Some data is cached locally on your device for offline access
  • Data Retention: We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide our services

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties except:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third parties who assist us in operating the App (e.g., Firebase)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Consent: When you explicitly consent to sharing

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct your information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability: Export your data in a readable format
  • Opt-out: Disable notifications or marketing communications

Data Export and Deletion

  • Data Export: You can request a copy of all your data through the App settings
  • Account Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data through the App
  • Data Retention: Deleted data is permanently removed from our servers

Children's Privacy

NoteXP is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

International Users

If you are using NoteXP from outside India, please note that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in India where our servers are located.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy in the App
  • Sending you an email notification
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
