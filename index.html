🔒 Introduction

Welcome to NoteXP! We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, processing, and protection when you use our mobile application NoteXP (the "App").

By using NoteXP, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

📷 Camera Permission Details

🎯 Camera Usage - QR Code Scanning Only

NoteXP requests camera permission exclusively for QR code scanning functionality. Here's exactly how we use it:

Specific Camera Uses

  • QR Code Scanning: To quickly subscribe to notice boards by scanning QR codes
  • Real-time Processing: Camera feed is processed in real-time without storage
  • No Image Capture: We do not capture, save, or store any photos or videos
  • No Face Recognition: We do not use facial recognition or biometric identification

Technical Implementation

  • Camera access is requested only when you choose to scan a QR code
  • Camera feed is processed locally on your device
  • No camera data is transmitted to our servers
  • Camera permission can be revoked at any time through device settings

🔒 Privacy Assurance

We guarantee that:

  • No photos or videos are ever saved to your device
  • No camera data is transmitted over the internet
  • Camera access is only active during QR scanning
  • You can disable camera permission without affecting other App features
